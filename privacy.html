Privacy Notice — budget-app

budget-app is a personal household budgeting app used by a single individual on their own phone. It is not offered to other people and it has no user accounts, no server, and no operator other than its owner.

Who is responsible

The owner of the app and of the bank accounts it reads. Contact: tjrgns97502@gmail.com.

What data is accessed

Account information (balances and transactions) from bank accounts that the owner has explicitly linked through the Enable Banking API under PSD2, and photographs of the owner's own shopping receipts.

Where the data goes

Into an SQLite database on the phone itself. There is no backend server and no analytics. Nothing is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising or profiling.

Receipt photos are read by on-device text recognition (Android ML Kit / Apple Vision); the images do not leave the phone. Optionally, the extracted product name strings alone may be sent to the Anthropic API to normalise them. Amounts, balances, account identifiers, and any other bank data are never sent anywhere.

Credentials

API credentials and the bank session identifier are held in the operating system's keystore (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). The app is locked behind biometric or device-credential authentication.

Retention

Data stays on the phone until the owner deletes it. Uninstalling the app removes the database. Bank access ends when the PSD2 consent expires (at most 90 days) or when the owner revokes it at their bank.

Legal basis and rights

The owner processes their own personal data for their own purely personal and household purposes. Access, rectification, erasure, and withdrawal of consent are exercised directly on the device and at the bank.
